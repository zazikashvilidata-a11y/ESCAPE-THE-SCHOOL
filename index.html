GAME GUIDE

Lives · Part 1 · Part 2 · Survival Tips

Lives (Whole Game)

❤️ ❤️ ❤️

You start the game with 3 lives — 3 hearts appear in the top right. If a monster or infected catches you, you lose 1 life and return to the last checkpoint. After returning, no one can catch you for 3 seconds.

When you run out of lives, the "BACK TO SCHOOL" screen appears with two buttons: +1 Life (15 R$) or Try Again.
00Prologue — Home → Classroom

Turn off the alarm, read the phone, get dressed, take the bag, and head to the bus stop.

Bus → School → Classroom: sit at your desk and listen to the teacher.

01Classroom — Papers & Lockers

Collect the papers — they contain the locker code. The key is in one of the lockers (one locker has a scary surprise). Open the door with the key.

02Hallway — Fuses & Janitor

Find 3 fuses and put them in the power box. Then take the keys from the janitor's cart and open the cafeteria door.

The janitor patrols the hallway. If he sees you, hide in a locker.

03Cafeteria — Recipe & Cook

Find the recipe — it has the fridge's 3-digit code. The hall key is inside the fridge.

When you take the key, the cook wakes up. You can buy items with coins at the buffet.

04Gym — Ventilation Shaft

Take the screwdriver, open the ventilation shaft, and crawl inside.

05Principal's Office — The Safe

Guess the safe code from the photos and the recording. The main key is in the safe — it opens the office door.

06Final Bell — Run from Grimm

Grimm chases you and always sees where you are, no matter where you hide. Just run to the exit with SHIFT.

★Ending — The Bus Ride

You sit on the bus, and the driver tells you: "School never ends..."

01Investigation — 3 Clues

Find 3 clues: a letter on the reception table; a laptop in your room on the desk; a diary on your bed.

Everything is stored in the journal (J).

02Planes — Green Fog

After the noise, look out the reception window. You'll see planes spraying green fog over the city.

03TV — The News

The news shows the infected count rising to 100. They grab people and drag them to schools.

04Memory — The Box

Dad tells you there's a box under the bed and the code is your birthday.

05Father's Box — Code 1407

It's in your parents' bedroom, under the bed. The code is 1407 — the hint is on the calendar hanging on the fridge. Inside is a pistol with 15 bullets.

06Invasion — Break the Window

The infected break the window. Run out the back door. If they get too close, hide in the closet — in your room or your parents' room.

07Street — Safe & Radio

The safe code is on a receipt on a trash container next to the Corner 24/7 store. The code changes every game. The safe is in the store's back room. Open it and take the radio.

The road west is blocked — quarantine, nothing there.

08Depot — Hold the Lever

Hold the lever at the generator. The gate opens in 24 seconds and 3 waves come during that time. These infected always know where you are, so shoot them.

★Wagon — The Truth

Play the video on the laptop. It turns out the principal organized everything to bring runaway students back to school. After that, "TO BE CONTINUED" appears.

!How to Survive
  • Infected can't see through walls. Use houses, cars, and containers as cover. The only exception is the waves at the gate.
  • Trash containers, closets, and toilet stalls are hiding spots. Hide with E. If you have a pursuer, they lose you immediately. To exit, press E or LEAVE.
  • The pistol doesn't kill — it only stuns an infected for 7 seconds. Shoot and run immediately.
  • Ammo is scarce: max 30. A yellow AMMO box gives +8 bullets and respawns in 45 seconds.
  • Escape with SHIFT: infected are fast, so don't run in a straight line. Use corners and try to hide.
  • With friends: one guards the lever at the gate, the others shoot.
  • On the next game, Part 2 will be unlocked and it will offer to start from there. A PART 2 button has been added to the Staff Room.
